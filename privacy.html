Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

UnimaxDL is built on the philosophy of user sovereignty and data minimalism. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal identity details to access any part of our service.

2. Information We Process

When you use UnimaxDL, we process minimal operational information solely to deliver the service:

  • Submitted Media URLs: Temporarily processed on-demand by our engine to extract formats and stream media chunks. Submitted URLs are not tied to personal identities.
  • Temporary Media Files: Any files processed on our servers are held in volatile temporary storage and automatically wiped after 30 minutes via scheduled cleanup.
  • Operational Telemetry: Anonymized metrics (such as target platform and download success rate) stored via Supabase to track system uptime and detect server errors.

3. Zero Advertising & Third-Party Trackers

Unlike conventional media downloaders, UnimaxDL operates without invasive advertising networks, pop-unders, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party behavioral trackers (such as Google AdSense or Facebook Pixel).

4. Local Storage Usage

We use browser localStorage solely to save user interface preferences, such as custom backend server endpoints and anonymous random session identifiers. No sensitive tracking tokens are placed in cookies.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us via our official GitHub repository or at privacy@unimaxdl.org.
