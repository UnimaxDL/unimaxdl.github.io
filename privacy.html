Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · UnimaxDL Open Source Project

1. Zero Accounts or Personal Data

UnimaxDL does not require registration, passwords, or personal profiles. We never sell or rent personal information to data brokers.

2. What Happens When You Paste a Link

Submitted media URLs are processed solely in volatile memory to resolve direct video and audio streams. Temporary server transfer buffers are purged automatically on a rolling 30-minute cleanup schedule.

3. Browser Preferences (`localStorage`)

Your browser stores only local utility preferences such as your chosen Light or Dark theme (udl-theme) and an optional local companion server address (udl_backend_url).

4. Ethical Partner Placements

Non-intrusive partner links and sponsored slots fund free hosting bandwidth for everyone. We keep all download inputs clean and free of misleading buttons.

5. Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to atomicdhrubaofficial@gmail.com or submitted publicly via our GitHub Repository Issues.
