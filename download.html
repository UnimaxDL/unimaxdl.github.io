Answers & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are 4K and 8K downloads exclusive to the Native App?

High-definition streams (1440p, 2160p, 4320p) on YouTube and other platforms separate the video and audio feeds into high-bitrate DASH streams. Merging multi-gigabyte video files in a web browser tab causes out-of-memory crashes. The native app uses your device's built-in GPU and local FFmpeg to combine streams instantly with zero server lag.

Is the UnimaxDL APK safe to install on my Android device?

Yes, 100%. UnimaxDL is open-source under the MIT license, meaning the full source code is publicly inspectable on GitHub. The APK contains zero analytics trackers, zero background services, and requires only standard internet and download storage permissions.

How do I update the application?

On PC and Android, UnimaxDL automatically checks for new releases on GitHub on startup. When an update is detected, an alert offers a 1-tap download of the latest release.

Does the native app run in the background when not in use?

No. When you close UnimaxDL, the process terminates completely. It does not spawn background daemons or drain your mobile battery.
