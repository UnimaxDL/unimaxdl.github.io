DMCA & Copyright Policy

TITLE 17 U.S.C. SECTION 512 NOTICE PROCEDURE // STATUS: ACTIVE

1. Statement of Non-Hosting & Passive Neutral Conduit

UniMaxDL adheres rigorously to Title 17, United States Code, Section 512 (the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, "DMCA").

NEUTRAL CONDUIT CLARIFICATION: UniMaxDL does NOT store, upload, broadcast, or maintain multimedia content or copyrighted intellectual property on its infrastructure. Our systems function exclusively as a technological routing conduit executing real-time extraction requests initiated on-demand by third-party end users.

2. Technological URL Blacklist Procedure

We respect the rights of creators and copyright holders worldwide. If you are a copyright owner or authorized representative and determine that your intellectual property is being indexed without permission via our extraction interface, you may submit a formal takedown request.

Upon receipt of a valid, actionable DMCA notice, our engineering team immediately adds the specific content identifiers or domain endpoints to our global resolution blacklist, permanently preventing further parsing through our public gateways.

3. Requirements for an Actionable Notice

Pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), an actionable takedown notice must contain:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed;
  2. Exact, verified URL(s) of the content to be placed on the technological blacklist;
  3. Legitimate contact details of the complainant (full legal name, physical address, phone number, and official corporate email);
  4. A statement affirming a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, agent, or the law;
  5. A statement under penalty of perjury that the information supplied is accurate and that the sender is authorized to act on behalf of the owner;
  6. An electronic or physical signature of the authorized copyright holder or legal counsel.

5. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe your content or submitted URL was mistakenly blacklisted as a result of misidentification, you may submit a formal counter-notification containing statutory details under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3).

6. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with DMCA provisions, UniMaxDL maintains a policy of terminating or blacklisting IP ranges or origin endpoints that repeatedly and systematically attempt to process infringing or unauthorized materials.
