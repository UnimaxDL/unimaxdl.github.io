Digital Millennium Copyright Act Notice

1. Statement of Non-Hosting & Passive Neutral Conduit

UnimaxDL operates in strict compliance with Title 17, United States Code, Section 512 (the Digital Millennium Copyright Act, "DMCA").

Important Notice: UnimaxDL does NOT host, archive, index, or store any copyrighted multimedia content on its servers. The Service functions solely as a client-side technological conduit utilizing publicly available tools to parse public URLs explicitly provided by the user.

2. DMCA Takedown & URL Blacklist Procedure

We respect the intellectual property rights of creators and copyright holders. If you are a copyright owner, or authorized to act on behalf of one, and you believe your copyrighted material is being accessed in violation of your rights via our indexing interface, you may submit a formal notification.

Upon receiving a verified notice, we will immediately implement technological safeguards to blacklist the specific URL(s) from being processed through our system.

3. Requirements for a Valid Notice

To submit a valid DMCA notice, please provide the following details:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed;
  2. Exact URL(s) of the material to be blocked;
  3. Your contact information (name, address, telephone number, and email address);
  4. A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner;
  6. A physical or electronic signature of the authorized copyright owner or representative.
