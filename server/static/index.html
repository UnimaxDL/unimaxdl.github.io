Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about UnimaxDL.

Is UnimaxDL completely free to use? ▼

Yes, 100% free! UnimaxDL is open-source software licensed under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or hidden fees.

Does it support 4K and 60FPS video downloads? ▼

Yes. Whenever the source platform provides 1440p, 4K UHD, or 8K streams, UnimaxDL automatically detects them and merges the best available video with high-definition audio into a clean MP4 file.

Can I download TikTok videos without watermark? ▼

Yes. UnimaxDL extracts the original, pristine high-definition video directly from TikTok's CDN without any overlay watermark or branding.

How do I connect the web app to my local PC server? ▼

Simply tap the ⚙️ gear icon in the top header. Select "Local PC Server", enter http://127.0.0.1:8000 (or your LAN IP), and save. Your downloads will process instantly on your PC!

Is downloading videos legal? ▼

Downloading content for personal offline archiving, fair use, or creative commons materials is legal in most jurisdictions. Users must respect copyright laws and the terms of service of each respective platform.
