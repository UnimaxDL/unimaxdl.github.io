Technical FAQ

Is UniMaxDL free and open-source?+
Yes. UniMaxDL is free software licensed under MIT. There are no subscription walls or download quotas.
Why do 4K videos and Reddit clips require audio-video muxing?+
Platforms like YouTube and Reddit split high-resolution video and audio into separate adaptive streams (DASH). UniMaxDL merges both tracks cleanly so your saved MP4 always includes full sound.
Are downloaded files retained on UniMaxDL servers?+
No. UniMaxDL acts strictly as a transient extraction conduit; temporary mux buffers are purged automatically.
