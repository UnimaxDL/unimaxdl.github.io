Common Questions

Why do 4K UHD & 60fps videos open the Free Native App option?

Most streaming sites store 4K video and high-bitrate audio as two separate streams that web browsers cannot combine natively without timing out. The free UnimaxDL Android APK and Desktop App merge them cleanly right on your own device.

Does UnimaxDL remove the TikTok watermark automatically?

Yes. Pasting any public TikTok link extracts the clean source video without the floating corner watermark, while also offering direct MP3 audio saving.

Why do Reddit videos saved elsewhere often have no sound?

Reddit separates video frames and audio tracks across two URLs. UnimaxDL's stream preparation automatically locks and merges both tracks so your clip plays back with synced sound.

Is UnimaxDL free to use on Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux?

Yes. Every tool, APK build, and desktop installer is free and MIT open source, funded ethically via transparent partner slots so users never face paywalls or subscriptions.
