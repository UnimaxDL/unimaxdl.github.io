Frequently Asked Questions

Is UnimaxDL free to use? +
Yes, 100% free with no subscription, no pop-up advertisements, and no registration required. The project is open-source under the MIT license.
Why are 4K and 8K downloads exclusive to the Native App? +
High-definition streams (1440p, 2160p, 4320p) on YouTube and other platforms separate video and audio into distinct DASH feeds. Muxing multi-gigabyte streams in a browser sandbox causes fatal memory limits. The native client leverages local GPU acceleration and static FFmpeg with zero server lag.
Can I extract 320kbps MP3 audio? +
Yes. The Audio tab isolates the highest-bitrate audio stream and provides studio-grade 320kbps MP3 or original lossless M4A/Opus downloads.
Does UnimaxDL log or store my downloads? +
No. UnimaxDL operates with zero user tracking, zero download logging, and zero telemetry. Stream resolution occurs client-side and via ephemeral proxy pipelines.
