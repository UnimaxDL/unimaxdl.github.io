Technical FAQ

Is UniMaxDL free and open-source? +
Yes. UniMaxDL is fully free software licensed under the MIT license. There are no paywalls, premium download subscriptions, or artificial bandwidth bottlenecks.
Why are 4K video streams downloaded with separate audio on some platforms? +
Modern platforms (including YouTube) serve high-resolution video (>1080p) via adaptive DASH streams where audio and video are hosted in distinct channels. UniMaxDL merges these streams on the fly with FFmpeg so you receive a unified file with full sound.
Are downloaded files stored permanently on UniMaxDL servers? +
No. UniMaxDL does not maintain a file database or long-term storage archive. The system acts as a transient technological conduit; files are temporarily processed in volatile RAM and purged after transfer.
