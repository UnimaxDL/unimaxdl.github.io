How to Download YouTube Videos

Save any YouTube clip or Short in 3 simple steps.

01

Copy YouTube URL

Copy the link of the YouTube video or Short from your browser address bar or the YouTube app share menu.

02

Paste in UnimaxDL

Paste the link into the search box above. Click Analyze to fetch available 4K, 1080p, and MP3 formats.

03

Download to Device

Choose your preferred quality (4K UHD, 1080p FHD, 720p HD, or MP3) and download directly to your drive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading YouTube videos.

Can I download YouTube videos in 4K resolution? +

Yes, UnimaxDL supports full 4K UHD (2160p), 1440p QHD, 1080p FHD, and 720p HD streams.

How do I convert YouTube videos to MP3 320kbps? +

Paste the YouTube URL, click Analyze, and select MP3 Studio Quality from the Audio tab.

Are YouTube Shorts supported? +

Yes, simply paste the Shorts link and download directly in vertical Full HD MP4.

Is there a download limit or cost? +

No, UnimaxDL is 100% free and open-source with no caps on download quantity or duration.

Can I batch download YouTube playlists? +

Yes, use the free UnimaxDL desktop companion app for multi-threaded batch and playlist downloads.
