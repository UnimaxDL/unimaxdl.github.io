Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading Twitch clips

Can I download deleted or subscriber-only Twitch VODs? +

UnimaxDL can download all publicly accessible clips and VODs. Videos that have been fully purged from Twitch servers or restricted to sub-only channels cannot be accessed without credentials.

What file format are Twitch clips saved in? +

All clips are saved in universally compatible H.264 MP4 with AAC audio, ready for editing in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, or sharing on Discord and TikTok.

Is there a watermark added to the video? +

No. UnimaxDL never stamps logos, watermarks, or overlays onto your downloaded video.
